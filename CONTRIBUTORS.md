## Work we learned from

- **AROS's `workbench.library` and `icon.library`**, read for how a replacement can be shaped (a thin library that hands the desktop to a process of its own). No code was taken.
- **Hyperion's NDK 3.2**: its SFD files, headers, autodocs and release notes are the interface we keep. They are used on the Team's machines and never committed.
